Power Systems Lab

Dearborn, MI, USA

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical, Electronics & Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan-Dearborn, advised by Prof. Wencong Su. My research sits at the intersection of power-system reliability and artificial intelligence, along two complementary directions:

  • AI for the grid — fine-tuned and multi-agent large language models and reinforcement learning as operational decision-makers: surrogate scheduling, economic dispatch, decentralized Volt/VAR control, and reliability-oriented clustering of DER-rich active distribution networks.
  • The grid for AI — integrating large-scale, uncertain AI data-center loads into distribution and transmission networks: hosting-capacity and reliability frameworks, grid-aware load coordination, and energy-storage strategies that expand hosting capacity while preserving reliability.

I also work on reliability assessment under high inverter-based-resource penetration, resilience of interdependent electricity, water, and transportation infrastructure, and game-theoretic models of carbon policy and second-life battery investment. I contribute to the NSF project Enhancing Grid Reliability and Stability with Distributed Energy Resources (Award #2321661).

In summer 2025 I was a Power System Engineering Intern with Danovo Energy Solutions (Quanta Technology), performing transmission planning and interconnection studies in PSLF, TARA, and PSS/E. Before Michigan, I received my M.Sc. from Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic) and my B.Sc. from K. N. Toosi University of Technology.

I review for IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid, IEEE Access, IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, Applied Energy, and other IEEE, IET, and Elsevier venues (150+ reviews).
