Fine-tuned and multi-agent large language models used as operational decision-makers for grid operation and optimization: surrogate scheduling for smart-building energy management, economic dispatch, decentralized Volt/VAR control, and reliability-oriented clustering of DER-rich active distribution networks, as scalable generative alternatives to conventional machine learning.

Related work: *Energy and Buildings* (2026), IEEE ECCE (2025), and two manuscripts under review at *IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid*.
