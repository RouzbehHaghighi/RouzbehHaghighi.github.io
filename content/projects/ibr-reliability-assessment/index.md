Inverter-based resources change what reliability means for a power system: low inertia, bidirectional flows, new failure modes in power electronics, and uncertainty that traditional indices do not capture. This project develops stochastic, probabilistic frameworks for reliability and resource-adequacy metrics on grids dominated by IBRs, as part of the NSF project “Enhancing Grid Reliability and Stability with Distributed Energy Resources” (Award #2321661).

Our survey in *Energies* reviewed reliability at the device, component, and system levels and argued that these levels must be analyzed together: converter reliability shapes system indices, and system conditions shape converter stress. It catalogs classic indices alongside those newly proposed for IBR-penetrated grids and sets out a multi-level research agenda, including benchmark systems, data sharing on converter failure behavior, and reliability software that coordinates components and layers.
