abstract: "This chapter reviews the genetic algorithm (GA), a population-based metaheuristic inspired by natural selection, and its application to two central power-system problems: generation expansion planning (GEP) and unit commitment (UC). After an overview of heuristic optimization in power systems, the chapter formulates GEP and UC, explains how each is encoded and solved with GA operators, and solves a sample GEP problem with the GA approach, discussing the results and the advantages of GA over gradient-based methods for integer-valued planning decisions."
