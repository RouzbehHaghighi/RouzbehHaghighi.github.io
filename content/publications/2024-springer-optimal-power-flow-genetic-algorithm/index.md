abstract: "This chapter presents the optimal power flow (OPF) problem and its solution with the genetic algorithm (GA). OPF is formulated as a constrained nonlinear optimization problem with equality and inequality constraints on bus voltages, generator outputs, and line flows. Evolutionary algorithms are compared with classical and mixed-integer approaches in terms of constraint handling, scalability, robustness, and flexibility. The application of GA to OPF is described step by step, and a sample OPF problem is solved with the GA approach, with the results presented and discussed."
