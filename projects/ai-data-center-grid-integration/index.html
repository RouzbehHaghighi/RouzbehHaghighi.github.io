Grid Integration of AI Data-Center Loads

Grid for AI

Related work: “Grid Integration of AI Data Centers: A Critical Review of Energy Storage Solutions,” Advances in Applied Energy, 2026, and “AI Data Center Load Modeling: A Timescale-Based Critical Review” (under review, Applied Energy).
