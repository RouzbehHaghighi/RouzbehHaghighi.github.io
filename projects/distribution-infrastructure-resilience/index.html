Resilience of Distribution and Interdependent Infrastructure

Sep 2026 · 1 min read
IEEE TPWRD
projects

Stochastic infrastructure damage evaluation with fragility curves and network reduction, resilience enhancement of distribution systems by remote-controlled switch placement, and resilience of the electricity–water–transportation nexus with last-mile solar relief pack delivery.

Related work: IEEE Transactions on Power Delivery (2024) and manuscripts under review at IEEE Transactions on Reliability and IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid.

Rouzbeh Haghighi
Ph.D. Candidate, Electrical & Computer Engineering
Rouzbeh Haghighi is a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical, Electronics & Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan-Dearborn, advised by Prof. Wencong Su. His research sits at the intersection of power-system reliability and artificial intelligence: large language models and reinforcement learning as operational decision-makers for the grid, and hosting-capacity and reliability frameworks for integrating large, uncertain AI data-center loads into distribution and transmission networks. In 2025 he carried out transmission planning and interconnection studies in PSLF, TARA, and PSS/E as a Power System Engineering Intern with Quanta Technology.
